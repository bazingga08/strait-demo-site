Privacy Policy: Strait Link

App: Strait Link (Android package com.straitlink.app) · Last updated: 3 October 2026

Strait Link is a test app for the Strait deep-linking service. It shows how links open the app and checks that each case works. It has no real user accounts, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. This policy explains exactly what the app and the Strait service process when you use it.

What is collected, and why

DataWhenWhy
The link you opened (short link and its destination)When a Strait link opens the appTo open the right screen and count the open
Device type details: platform, device class (phone/tablet), major OS version, in-app browser nameWhen you tap a Strait linkLink analytics and choosing app, store or website
Network address, shortened to its first three parts (for example 203.0.113.x); the full IP address is never storedWhen you tap a link or the app contacts StraitLink analytics and matching an install to the link tapped
Screen width, pixel ratio, language and time zone, combined into a short code ("fingerprint")On first open after install, and when you use the Fingerprint check screenTo recognise the link you tapped before installing (deferred deep linking). A match can only happen within 1 hour of the tap
The Google Play install referrer (contains the Strait link ID)On first open after installing from Google PlayTo open the link you tapped before installing
Test events such as "purchase" with an amountOnly when you tap the test buttonsTo demonstrate conversion analytics. No payment is made and no payment data exists

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, advertising ID or any account credentials. The "Log in" button in the app is a simulation; nothing is sent.

Stored only on your phone

Checklist results, the simulated login state and the app's settings are stored on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

Who processes the data

  • Strait (the service operator) receives the data above through its link server.
  • Render hosts the link server, and Supabase hosts its database. They act as service providers and process data only to run the service.
  • Google Play provides the install referrer under Google's own privacy policy.

Data is not sold, not shared with advertisers, and not used to track you across other apps or websites.

Security and retention

All traffic uses HTTPS. Device "fingerprint" codes can only be matched for 1 hour after a link is tapped and are never linked to your identity. Link, open and test-event records are kept for analytics while this test service runs, and are deleted on request.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To ask for deletion of server records, or with any privacy question, email privacy@straitlink.in.

Children

The app is a developer test tool, is not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
